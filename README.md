## Business Systems & Compliance Analyst
**AI-driven event ticketing platform** · Most recent role

I worked on a live event ticketing platform in the same space as Posh and Eventbrite. Promoters create events, set pricing, and track performance through AI-backed analytics, while attendees buy tickets and pay at checkout. I validated the data and logic behind those workflows: testing updates with the developer, checking system output against real transactions, documenting issues in Jira, and verifying every fix before sign-off.

### Analytics validation: promoter analytics, backed by AI
Promoters rely on the platform's AI-backed analytics to judge how their events are performing and decide on pricing, promotion, and planning.

- **Reconcile:** Compared what the analytics dashboard reported with the underlying ticket sales and orders to confirm the figures were accurate.
- **Challenge:** Looked for AI-generated insights that were inconsistent, unclear, or unsupported by the underlying data.
- **Document:** Logged discrepancies in Jira with the data that showed the problem, so the developer could find the cause quickly.
- **Re-verify:** Re-checked the analytics after each update to confirm fixes held and nothing else shifted.

*Why it mattered:* if the figures are wrong, every pricing and promotion decision built on them is wrong too.

### Case study: Apple Pay failures at checkout
**Business problem:** Attendees couldn't pay with Apple Pay, so ready-to-buy customers were failing to complete purchases.

- **Investigate:** Created my own attendee account and walked through checkout as a real buyer to reproduce the failure.
- **Report:** Logged the issue in Jira with steps, expected versus actual behavior, and context for the developer.
- **Validate:** After the fix, re-ran the full purchase with Apple Pay and confirmed the payment and order completed correctly.

*Why it mattered:* a broken payment method is direct revenue leakage for promoters and for the platform.

### Case study: 75% promo code not applying
**Business problem:** A promoter set up a 75% discount for their event, but it didn't apply at checkout.

- **Investigate:** Tested both sides: confirmed the code's setup as the promoter, then used an attendee account to see how checkout calculated the total.
- **Report:** Documented the exact setup and the checkout result in Jira to help trace where the discount logic broke.
- **Validate:** Re-ran checkout after the fix and verified the discounted total was calculated correctly.

*Why it mattered:* pricing that doesn't match what the promoter configured hurts sales and trust in the platform.

### Compliance & controls
- **Payment integrity:** Confirming supported payment methods complete correctly and orders match what was charged.
- **Pricing accuracy:** Making sure discounts calculate as configured, so buyers pay what they were promised.
- **Audit trail:** Keeping issues, fixes, and re-tests recorded in Jira to show what changed and when it was verified.

**Tools & skills:** Jira, Excel, data validation, reconciliation, root-cause analysis, AI output review, payment and pricing logic testing, issue documentation

*Why it mattered:* in legal work, a missing document, a wrong payment record, or a stalled case file creates real risk for the client and the firm.
